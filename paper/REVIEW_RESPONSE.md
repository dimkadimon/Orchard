# Response to the November 2026 review

This is a research-draft response, not a claim of author approval or completed peer review.

1. **Real-coordinate optimality.** The construction table and proposition remain explicitly lower bounds, not real maxima. The independent determinant census was rerun for all nine medium-size entries and the n=11, 13, 14 and 15 examples. It agrees with the primary verifier. Reproducibility instructions and dependencies are now explicit. An exact finite census establishes the score of a supplied set, not the optimality or novelty of that score.
2. **n=14 and n=15.** The new Proposition 4.1 proves that a hypothetical 14-point set with 11 rich lines or 15-point set with 13 rich lines has no line of six or more points. It also bounds the number of other rich lines that can contain four outside points when there is a five-point line. OEIS A006065 and MathWorld report the exactly-four values 10 and 12, respectively; the manuscript distinguishes these secondary-source reports from its unconditional at-least-four bounds. Conditional on those reported upper bounds, the remaining case requires a five-point line. A new exact, restricted 5-by-5 grid search found no example; its scope is documented and it does not prove nonexistence. The unconditional intervals remain [10,11] and [12,13].
3. **Asymptotic upper bound.** The revision explains why the balanced second-order test alone is asymptotically too weak to improve 1/14: its left side is O(n³) whereas the line-pair budget is Θ(n⁴) for Θ(n²) rich lines. No improved asymptotic upper bound is claimed.
4. **Novelty and sources.** The review describes some results as novel. This has not been established by an exhaustive priority search, so the manuscript does not endorse that assertion. It explicitly limits its claims, retains credit to Li for the underlying construction, and distinguishes the integer contest from both real and unrestricted variants. MathWorld's exactly-four table was added as a corroborating secondary source, not as a proof of the at-least-four results.
5. **Draft and AI-assistance status.** The earlier revision included an approval caveat. The author subsequently requested its removal and a dedicated AI-attribution section, which has now been added. This change does not assert that an independent review has taken place.
6. **Figures and verification.** The previously improved high-contrast configuration figures are retained. Exact arithmetic and full line histograms are documented, and the independent verifier's shared dependency and inability to check cross-ratios are disclosed. The PDF has been rebuilt. Review of the proof and sources by the author or an independent specialist is still recommended.
