## Scope

The submission draft excludes the previously published 24-point/18-line five-orchard, known small orchard values, and unrelated exploratory searches. It retains only the putative contributions: the dual bound and small-case comparison against classical Melchior, four explicitly certified 28-, 36-, 40- and 48-point ring constructions, five 42–46 point extensions, and a long-line reduction. Prior results are cited where necessary for context or comparison. Existing repository reports are not part of the upload bundle.
